In Search campaigns, Google lets you set Google Search, Google search partners, and the Google Display Network to different priority levels. By default, all channels are set to High priority.

High priority channels get budget first. Low priority channels get what is left. For a Low priority channel, you can set a daily budget allocation percentage. Zero percent means Google will not spend on that channel.

So a Search campaign you thought ran only on Google may also be showing on Search Partner websites you never picked and never checked. A click from a partner site is not a click from a person searching on Google. It can be cheaper. It can look good in the report. But it rarely turns into a call or a booked job.

The fix is one setting. Set Google Search to High. Set Search Partners to Low, with a small budget cap. Five percent to test it. Or zero percent to stop it.

And check your report. Segment by Network. See what Search Partners actually brought you. Calls or just clicks. If it never brought a call, do not pay for it again.
