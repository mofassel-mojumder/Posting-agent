From December 4, Google will take photos from your website and turn them into your Performance Max ads. By default.

Even if you turned Dynamic images off. Google ignores that setting now, for new campaigns. Existing campaigns keep their settings. Only new ones change.

That means a blurry van photo, a messy job site photo, a random team photo. Any of them can show as your ad, to people ready to hire you. And you still pay for every click.

The fix takes two minutes. When you build the new campaign, open the Images setting and turn Landing page images off. Then upload only the photos you chose.

You control the image. Not Google.
