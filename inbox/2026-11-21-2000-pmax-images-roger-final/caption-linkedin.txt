From December 4, 2026, every new Performance Max campaign pulls images from your landing pages by default, even if you turned Dynamic images off for the whole account.

Google ignores that setting now, for new campaigns. Existing campaigns keep their settings. Only new ones change.

For a local service business, that is dangerous. A blurry van photo, a messy job site photo, a random team photo. Any of them can show as your ad, to people ready to hire you. And you still pay for every click.

Before you launch, open the Images setting and turn Landing page images off. Then upload only the photos you chose.

You control the image. Not Google.
