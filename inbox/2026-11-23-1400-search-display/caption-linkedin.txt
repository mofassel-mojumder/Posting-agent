In February 2027, Google moves every Search campaign with Display Expansion turned on to channel prioritization. The current spend allocation setup goes away, and you may see a shift in channel spending between Google Search, search partners and the Google Display Network.

Today Google splits the budget in the background. You cannot see the split. You cannot change it.

Under the new system, every channel starts as High priority. Search. Search partners. Display. All High. So Display can take more of your Search budget than it does today.

For a local service business, that is real money. A plumber paying for clicks from people who were never searching. A roofer paying for banner views. No calls. Just spend.

The fix is one setting. Open your Search campaign. Set the Display Network to Low priority, with a 50 percent daily budget allocation. That keeps the split you have today. Or turn Display off, if you never wanted it.

And check one more thing. If your campaign uses Target impression share, Display stops serving there in February.

Do this before February. Not after.
