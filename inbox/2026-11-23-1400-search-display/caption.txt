Your Search budget is about to leak into Display. And Google will not ask you first.

You run a Search campaign. And the Display Network is turned on. Maybe you turned it on. Maybe it was on by default. Either way, your Search budget is shared.

Today Google splits it in the background. You cannot see the split. You cannot change it.

In February 2027, that split goes away. Google moves every Search campaign with Display on to a new system. Channel prioritization. Every channel starts as High priority. Search. Search partners. Display. All High.

So Display can take more of your Search budget than it does today. And you will see the spend shift. Without anyone deciding it.

For a local service business, that is real money. A plumber paying for clicks from people who were never searching. A roofer paying for banner views. No calls. Just spend.

The fix is one setting. Open your Search campaign. Set the Display Network to Low priority. With a 50 percent daily budget allocation. That keeps the split you have today. Or turn Display off, if you never wanted it.

And check one more thing. If your campaign uses Target impression share, Display stops serving there in February.

Do this before February. Not after.
