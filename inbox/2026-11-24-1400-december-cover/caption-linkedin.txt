December is the busiest month for roofers. Storm damage. Emergency leaks. Everyone calls at the same time. And the roofer is already on a roof. Hands full. Phone ringing in his pocket. He cannot answer it.

His ad is still running. Google is still taking his money. But nobody is watching it. That call goes to the next roofer. And the job is gone. Not because his work is bad. Because nobody was there to catch it.

December is not the month to pause your ads. It is the month your ads need someone watching them. Checking the search terms. Making sure the money goes to storm damage and emergency leaks, not wasted clicks. Setting up the tracking and the follow-up, so a missed call does not mean a lost job.

While you fix the roof, someone has to watch the business.
