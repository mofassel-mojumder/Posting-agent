Google just turned AI Max ON for you. By default.

You build a new Search campaign in Editor, and Google can change your ads, expand your final URL, and show you for searches you never picked.

For a local business, that means clicks from towns you do not serve, for jobs you do not do.

Fix: open the new campaign, turn AI Max OFF, turn text automation OFF, turn final URL expansion OFF.

You control the spend. Not Google.
