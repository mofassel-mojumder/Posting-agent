December is the busiest month for electricians. EV chargers. Emergency call-outs. Everyone wants it done before Christmas. And the electrician is already on a job. Hands full. Phone ringing in his pocket. He cannot answer it.

His ad is still running. Google is still taking his money. But nobody is watching it. That call goes to the next electrician. And the job is gone. Not because his work is bad. Because nobody was there to catch it.

December is not the month to pause your ads. It is the month your ads need someone watching them. Checking the search terms. Making sure the money goes to EV charger installs and emergency call-outs, not wasted clicks. Setting up the tracking and the follow-up, so a missed call does not mean a lost job.

While you wire the house, someone has to watch the business.
