Google Ads Editor 2.14 quietly changed the default for new Search campaigns.

AI Max, text asset automation, and Final URL expansion are now ON by default.

That means Google can rewrite your ads, send clicks to pages you did not choose, and match you to searches you never picked.

For local service businesses, the cost is real: clicks from towns you do not serve, for jobs you do not do.

Before you launch, check three toggles in Editor: AI Max, text automation, final URL expansion. Turn them OFF unless you chose them on purpose.

You control the spend. Not Google.
